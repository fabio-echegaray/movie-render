────────────────────────────────────────────

              unnecessary path injection and unused `import os`/`import sys`.

  [DONE] 11. Replace 22 `assert` statements with proper exceptions — assert

  [DONE] 16b. MovieRender's `PixelTools` copies all `__dict__` entries
              (`pixel_tools.py:5-10`) — fragile duck-typing that leaks
